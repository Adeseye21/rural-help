Rural Help Design System

Colors, typography, buttons, form elements, cards, and complete example screens. Mobile-first and built for people with limited technical experience.

Foundations

Color palette

Green reads as health and calm without feeling clinical. Soft green supports, dark green anchors headings, and yellow is reserved for attention that is not an emergency.

Foundations

Typography

Inter is the single family used everywhere. Headings are bold and short. Body text stays at 16px or larger with generous line height so it stays readable on small, low-quality screens.

Heading level 1 — 28px bold

Heading level 2 — 22px bold

Heading level 3 — 18px bold

Heading level 4 — 16px bold

Body large — 17px. Used for the most important sentence on a screen, such as what the patient should do next.

Body default — 16px. Regular reading size for guidance, summaries, and instructions.

Body small — 15px. Used inside cards and list items where space is tight.

Caption — 13px. Timestamps, hints, and disclaimers only. Never for instructions a patient must follow.

Component 09

First-aid and guidance card

Numbered steps in plain language, followed by a short list of what not to do. Condition-gated, so only relevant steps appear.

First aid: severe bleeding

Use when blood is heavy or will not stop.

  1. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and keep pressing without lifting it.
  2. If blood soaks through, add another cloth on top. Do not remove the first one.
  3. Lay the person down and raise the injured area if this does not hurt more.
  4. Keep the person warm and calm while help is on the way.

Do not:

  • Do not pull out an object stuck in the wound.
  • Do not wash or push anything into a deep wound.
This is basic first-aid information. It does not replace training or professional care.
Component 10

Warning and emergency alerts

Three levels. Emergency uses the red border and demands action now. Urgent uses yellow and means contact a professional today. Info uses green and carries no alarm.

Severe chest pain Severe chest pain could be a sign of a heart problem. Do not wait — get emergency professional care now.
Please contact a professional today A stiff neck together with fever needs urgent assessment. Do not delay because of this app.
Saved on this device Your emergency information is stored on this phone. You decide what is shared and with whom.

Foundations

Accessibility rules

These are not optional. They are the reason the interface works for a first-time user on an older phone.

  • ✓Body text is 16px or larger with 1.6 line height, so it stays readable on small screens.
  • ✓Every tap target is at least 48px tall.
  • ✓Focus states use a 3px green ring, never a removed outline.
  • ✓Status is always shown with words as well as colour, never colour alone.
  • ✓Emergency red is reserved for conditions needing immediate professional care.
  • ✓Plain language throughout. Sentences are short and instructions use verbs first.
  • ✓The AI-versus-professional distinction is always stated on guidance output.
