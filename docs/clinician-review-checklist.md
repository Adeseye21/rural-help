# Rural Help — Clinician Review Checklist

**Purpose:** systematic sign-off of every health-guidance string the app shows to patients, before field testing or pilot.
**How to use:** for each numbered item, read the app's exact wording (copied verbatim from the code — nothing paraphrased), then mark one verdict and add a comment where it is not correct.

- ✅ **Correct** — safe as written.
- ⚠️ **Too strong** — alarms more than warranted (sends people on long, costly trips for something routine).
- 🚨 **Too weak** — understates something dangerous (the harmful direction — this is what the review is hunting for).

**Review in this order:** Part A (emergencies) first, then the fever-with-body-aches pathway (B10), then the rest. Those carry the highest risk if wrong.

**When finished:** complete the sign-off block at the end. "Signed off for testing" and "signed off for pilot" are two separate decisions.

## Part D — First-aid guides (shown in emergencies)

The note above every guide: *"Rural Help suggestion: this is basic first-aid information. It does not replace training or professional care. In an emergency, get professional help first."*

### D1. Severe bleeding — use when blood is spurting, flowing heavily, or will not stop.

Steps: press firmly with a clean cloth and keep pressing without lifting; if blood soaks through, add another cloth on top, do not remove the first; lay the person down and raise the injured area if it does not hurt more; keep warm and calm. Avoid: pulling out stuck objects (press around instead); washing or pushing anything into a deep wound.
