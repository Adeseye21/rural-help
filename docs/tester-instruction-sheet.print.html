What to do inside the app

  1. Create your own account with your name, an email address, and a password you choose. (This account is yours — it is different from the username and password above.)
  2. Type "I have leg ache" and answer the questions the app asks you.
  3. Go back. Type "I have a fever and body ache all over" and answer those questions.
  4. Save that conversation. Then find it again.
  5. Find where to get help nearby (clinics or facilities).

After you finish, please answer these

  1. What is this app for, in your own words?
  2. Was there any word or sentence you did not understand? Which one?
  3. Was anything slow? What were you doing then?
  4. Did anything scare or confuse you? What?
  5. Did you trust the advice it gave? Why or why not?
  6. If you felt very sick at night with no transport, what would you do with this app?
  7. Who in your house would use this? Who could not? Why?
  8. What one thing should change?
If you get stuck: stop, note down where you got stuck and what the screen showed, and tell the person who gave you this sheet. Do not worry — getting stuck is also a result.
